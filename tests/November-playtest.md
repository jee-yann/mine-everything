## Compare two ore samples

A mineral rolls one of six grades when you mine it. Dirt and stone have a fixed price. Without bonuses, the mining prompt and Index show 12–24 gold for copper. A 50% copper sample sells for 12 gold and a 100% sample sells for 24. When you sell one item from a mixed stack, the counter quotes the lowest grade first. Crafting follows the same order. Your best grade stays in the index after you empty your bag.

## Try the new supplies

| Item | Craft cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick-cut wax | 3 coal and 2 stone | Half the normal cut time for twelve successful cuts. |
| Diamond teeth | 2 iron and 8 gems | Six very fast plain-stone cuts. You still need enough pick power for the depth. |
| Cave pulse | 2 copper and 3 gems | Opens up to twelve nearby stone blocks and awards no loot or XP. |
| Pet's ore compass | 3 copper and 1 iron | An equipped pet turns toward ore within five blocks when it has a clear line of sight from the player. |

The stamp card, daily orders and **Jobs → Time in the cove** pay gems. Claim 1, 2 and 4 gems after 10, 20 and 60 cumulative minutes in the game each UTC day. Rejoining a saved game resumes that day's timer. Missing a day keeps the stamps already collected on the seven-day card. The seventh stamp pays 15 gems. Pet spins and a full spawn rebuild were discussed as future work.
